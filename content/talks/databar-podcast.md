In this episode of De Databar (Dutch), I talk about the background of Polars, what makes it fast, and what went into writing the O'Reilly book with Jeroen Janssens.

Topics covered:

- **History of Polars:** How Polars started at Xomnia and grew into one of the fastest DataFrame libraries available.
- **Performance advantages:** Why Polars outperforms Pandas on most workloads and how the Lazy API contributes to that.
- **Competing frameworks:** How Polars compares to Spark, DuckDB, and other modern data tools.
- **Polars Cloud:** What distributed Polars execution looks like and where the project is headed.
- **LLMs and Polars:** How large language models handle Polars code today and what that means for developers.
- **The book:** What drove us to write a comprehensive guide and what readers can expect.

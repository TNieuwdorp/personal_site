Jeroen Janssens and I joined Joe Reis to talk about all things Polars: where it came from, why it is worth switching to, and how we approached writing the book.

Topics covered:

- **Evolution of Polars:** From a side project at Xomnia to a widely adopted DataFrame library.
- **Why switch from Pandas:** The performance and ergonomics case for Polars in real data pipelines.
- **Writing the book:** How Jeroen and I structured the content and what was hardest to explain.
- **Real-world adoption:** What we have seen from teams migrating large codebases.
